OUR HERITAGE & PROPRIETORS

24 Years of Retail Leadership in Martur

Founded in 2002 under the visionary leadership of Ramineni Veeraiah & Ramineni Srinivasarao, BHARATH FOOT WEAR has grown from a local store into the most trusted footwear sanctuary in Martur, Andhra Pradesh.

Built upon the simple promise of "The Perfect Fit for Everyone", we curate genuine collections from India's leading footwear brands, ensuring every child, worker, athlete, and senior citizen finds absolute comfort and durability.
